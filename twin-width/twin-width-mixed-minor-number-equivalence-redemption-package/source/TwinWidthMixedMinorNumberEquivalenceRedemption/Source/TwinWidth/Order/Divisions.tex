\(\mathrm{Division}(n,k)\) is the type of ordered \(k\)-divisions of
\(\{0,\ldots,n-1\}\).  It consists of a family of nonempty finite parts indexed
by \(\mathrm{Fin}\,k\), together with proofs that the parts are pairwise
disjoint, cover all of \(\mathrm{Fin}\,n\), are convex intervals in the natural
order, and are ordered so that every element of an earlier part is less than
every element of a later part.
