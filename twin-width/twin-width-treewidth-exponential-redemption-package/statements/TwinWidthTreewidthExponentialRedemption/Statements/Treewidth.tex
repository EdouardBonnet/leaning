\(\operatorname{treewidth}(G)\) is the least natural number \(w\) such that
there exists \(D:\operatorname{TreeDecomposition}(G)\) with
\[
  \operatorname{treeDecompositionWidth}(D)\le w.
\]
Equivalently, it is
\[
  \operatorname{leastNat}\bigl(w\mapsto
    \exists D:\operatorname{TreeDecomposition}(G),\,
      \operatorname{treeDecompositionWidth}(D)\le w\bigr).
\]
